About me

Hey, I'm Andrew

I've spent over a decade as a product design leader, building teams, mentoring designers, and helping people grow into the work and lives they actually want.

I'm a professionally trained life and career coach and a Co-Active Practitioner (CAP). It's a coaching method that's curious, direct, and human, and it fits how I already work.

Outside of coaching, I'm a deeply creative person. I run Cake Therapy, my home bakery, and spend time throwing pottery and shooting film photography. Making things keeps me curious, and it shows up in how I coach.

There is a longer version of how I got here, and why I left my job to do this. Read my story.
