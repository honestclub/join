What we work on

Deciding well, not just deciding fast

Most career advice starts with the résumé. That is the last step, and it is the easy one. The harder work is getting honest about what you are moving toward, so the move is one you would make again.

Getting honest about the why

Before any of the tactics, the question that actually matters: what are you moving toward, and what are you moving away from?

Deciding without spiralling

We slow the decision down far enough that you can hear yourself think over the noise of everyone's opinions.

Telling your story

Naming what you have done in a way that is true to you and still lands with the people you want to reach.

This is the part that is genuinely hard to do on your own.
