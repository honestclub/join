This agreement is between Andrew Tolentino, DBA Honest Club (“Coach,” “I”) and the person who completes checkout (“Client,” “you”). It covers every Honest Club plan: a Single Session, a Coaching Package, or Monthly Coaching. Where a section only applies to one plan, it says so.

It takes effect when you agree to it at checkout and complete payment. The plan, rate, and amount on your checkout page and Stripe receipt are part of this agreement, and your receipt is your record of it.

1Coaching, and what it isn’t

Coaching is a collaborative, forward-looking conversation to help you gain clarity and act on your goals. I’m trained through CTI’s Co-Active program (CAP credential). It is not therapy, counseling, medical care, substance-abuse treatment, legal advice, or financial or investment advice. I don’t diagnose or treat anything, nothing I say substitutes for a licensed professional, and you shouldn’t stop or change treatment based on our conversations. If another service would serve you better, I’ll say so.

Every decision you make is yours, as are the outcomes. If you’re in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, contact emergency services or call or text 988. I’m not an emergency service and I’m not available for urgent support between sessions. I’ll show up prepared and honest; you’ll show up on time, do the work between sessions, pay on schedule, and tell me when something isn’t working. You confirm you’re at least eighteen.

Single Session and Coaching Package

Sessions expire. Every session must be held within your term. Anything unused at the end of it is forfeited unless we agree in writing to extend, and booking within the term is on you. Package sessions usually happen every one to two weeks.

Monthly Coaching

This is a flat monthly fee for ongoing coaching every other week, not a set number of sessions, so how many land in a given billing period follows the calendar. Skipped weeks don’t stack up. Sessions you don’t take aren’t banked, credited, or refunded.

3Payment

You pay the amount shown at checkout for the plan and rate you choose, by card or ACH through Stripe. Every rate gets the same coaching. Coaching begins once payment is received.

Single Session and Coaching Package

Paid in full at checkout, before the first session. This is a one-time payment. There’s no subscription and no recurring charge.

Monthly Coaching

Automatic renewal: please read

Monthly Coaching renews by itself. By completing checkout, you authorize Honest Club to charge your payment method the monthly amount shown at checkout today, and again automatically on the same day of every month after that, with no further notice and no end date, until you cancel. If that day doesn’t exist in a month, you’re charged on the last day of that month.

Cancel any time by emailing andrew@honestclub.co or through the billing link in your Stripe receipt. Cancelling takes effect at the end of your current billing period, the day before your next charge date (Section 5).

Your card or bank account stays on file with Stripe. I’ll give you thirty (30) days’ notice before any price change. If a payment fails and isn’t resolved within seven (7) days, sessions pause until it clears.

All plans

Billing questions come to me first. You agree to give me ten (10) business days to sort it out before filing a chargeback. Disputing sessions already held is a breach, and you stay responsible for the amount plus processor fees. Fees exclude taxes. Invoice and W-9 on request.

4Scheduling & missed sessions

Give me 24 hours’ notice to reschedule and the session doesn’t count as used. A session cancelled later than that, or missed entirely, counts as delivered and isn’t refunded, credited, or rescheduled. Being more than fifteen minutes late without a word may be treated as a no-show. You get one grace exception, and if it becomes a pattern we’ll talk about it honestly. If I cancel, I’ll reschedule at your convenience and it never counts against you.

5Ending or cancelling

Single Session

If you cancel with at least 24 hours’ notice and would rather not reschedule, I’ll refund your payment in full. After that, Section 4 applies.

Coaching Package

Either of us can end the package at any time, in writing. Sessions unused in the first half of your package are refundable pro-rata. Those in the second half are not, and sessions that expire at the end of the term are forfeited.

Monthly Coaching

Cancel any time by emailing andrew@honestclub.co or through the billing link in your Stripe receipt. No notice period, no fee, no explanation needed. Future charges stop and your membership continues through the end of your current billing period, which runs up to the day before your next charge date. For example, if you subscribed on the 20th and cancel on the 5th, coaching continues through the 19th and you aren’t charged again. Monthly fees are earned when charged and aren’t refunded: a partial billing period isn’t pro-rated, and sessions you didn’t take aren’t refunded or credited. That’s the trade for month-to-month flexibility with no minimum. If I end your membership, I’ll refund the pro-rated portion of the current billing period for sessions not yet held.

All plans

If I believe coaching isn’t the right fit for what you need, I’ll tell you directly, help you find a better resource, and refund everything you’ve paid for and haven’t used.

6Confidentiality

What we discuss stays between us, and I won’t share it with your employer or anyone else without your written permission. Three exceptions: if I reasonably believe you’re at risk of harm to yourself or others; if required by law, subpoena, or court order; and with my coaching supervisor, anonymized. Coaching isn’t legally privileged the way it is with an attorney or therapist, so I can’t promise a court won’t compel disclosure. Neither of us records a session, including with AI note-takers, without the other’s permission, and I won’t use your name or story in marketing without your written consent.
