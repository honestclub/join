About me

Hey, I'm Andrew

I spent about twenty years in product design, the last five or so leading teams and mentoring designers. I loved the work, and I noticed the part I loved most was helping people grow into the work and lives they actually wanted.

So I left to do that full time. I'm a professionally trained life and career coach and a Co-Active Practitioner (CAP): curious, direct, and very human.

Outside of coaching I bake, throw pottery, and shoot film. Fair warning: I'm loud, I laugh a lot, and I get genuinely excited about your stuff. Bring your whole self, because I'm bringing mine.

Good questions

Before you book

Is it really free?

Yes. No card, no catch. The free session is how we both find out if working together makes sense.

Is this a sales call in disguise?

No. Most of the time is spent actually coaching you on what's on your mind. In the last few minutes I'll share how my coaching works and what it costs, and you can take it or leave it.

What if I don't know what I want yet?

That's the most common place people start. Figuring out what you want is part of the work, and you don't need a tidy story to show up.

Where do sessions happen?

On video, so you can join from anywhere. I'm based in San Francisco, and I coach people wherever they are.

I'm not a designer or in tech. Can I still book?

Absolutely. Most of my clients are, but the work is human. If something here resonates, let's talk.
