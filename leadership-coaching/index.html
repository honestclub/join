What we work on

The parts of leading nobody trains you for

Leadership training tends to cover frameworks. It rarely covers the moment before a hard conversation, or what to do when the version of yourself you brought to work stops feeling like you. That is where coaching earns its place.

Feedback that lands

Saying the true thing clearly, without softening it until there is nothing left in it.

Your own voice as a leader

Borrowed styles wear off eventually. We look for the one you can actually sustain.

Boundaries and capacity

What you take on, what you hand over, and what you are finally allowed to put down.

This is the part that is genuinely hard to do on your own.
