Three ways I work inside companies

Executive coaching

One on one coaching for executives and senior leaders, sponsored by the organization. The higher someone goes, the fewer people tell them the truth. I sit outside the org chart, which means I can.

For companies investing in the people carrying the most weight.

Founder coaching

Coaching for founders and entrepreneurs in the early, uncertain part. At this stage the company and the person are effectively the same thing, which is both the whole problem and the whole opportunity.

Consulting and coaching with a whole team. Where the friction actually sits, what nobody is saying in the room, and how a group of genuinely good people can start working like one.
