What I offer

Two ways to go about it

Both packages run one on one, or in a small group where you find out fairly quickly that you are not the only one carrying this. The difference between them is not intensity. It is how far you want to climb.

Short path: 1 to 3 months

The Landing

  • One on one or small group

A short, focused stretch for one specific thing. A transition you are already in the middle of, a decision you keep circling, a change you can feel coming but cannot name yet. We clear enough space to see it properly, and then you move.

Best for career transitions, a role change, or a life shift you want to handle well rather than survive.

Longer growth: 3 to 12 months

The Summit

  • One on one or small group
  • Full transformation

The long version, and the steeper one. Not a single decision but the whole shape of how you work and live. There is time here to get past the first honest answer to the one underneath it, to build real mastery in how you lead and create, and to let the change actually set.

Best for full transformation and for growth you intend to hold for years rather than weeks. The deepest work I do.
