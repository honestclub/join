The path

What the work looks like

  • 01

    A free 25-minute call

    No prep needed. We talk, you get a feel for how I work, and at the end I'll share how coaching with me works so you can decide if it lands.

  • 02

    We get honest

    We choose the shape, monthly, six months, or a single session, and start saying the true things out loud, including the parts you have been carefully talking around.

  • 03

    The change holds

    Roles and titles shift when something inside shifts first. In my experience, that shift shows up around the eighth session, so we stay with it until you can hear yourself clearly without me.

Twenty-five minutes, no cost, and you get a real session out of it. We will talk through packages and pricing there too.
