What the research says

Coaching for physician burnout has evidence behind it

In November 2025, Annals of Internal Medicine published a review of 99 randomized trials testing what actually helps healthcare workers with burnout. For physicians, professional coaching measurably reduced burnout and depersonalization, the numbness where patients start to feel like tasks.

Weeks: programs that ran longer worked better

Published in Annals of Internal Medicine

The review compared individual-level programs across physicians, nurses and midwives, and mixed groups of healthcare staff. The pattern was clear. Professional coaching helped physicians, while mindfulness programs helped nurses, midwives, and mixed teams more than they helped physicians. And for both, the benefit was stronger when the program ran for more than four weeks.

That last finding shapes how I work. One workshop or a single wellness day is rarely enough. What seems to help is a steady, ongoing conversation with someone whose only job is to be in your corner.

In the spirit of the name: most trials in the review weren't blinded and relied on self-reported measures, which is hard to avoid in this kind of research. And if you're a nurse, the evidence points more strongly to mindfulness than to coaching. The two aren't competing, and plenty of people do both.

Source: Collett G, Gupta A, et al. Efficacy of individual-level interventions to mitigate the risk for burnout among health care professionals. Annals of Internal Medicine, 2025. Summarized by Medscape.

Questions

What people usually ask first

Does coaching actually help physician burnout?

The best current evidence says yes. The 2025 review of 99 trials found coaching reduced burnout and depersonalization among physicians, with programs over four weeks working best.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching looks forward and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If you're struggling with depression or thoughts of harming yourself, please reach a clinician first. The free Physician Support Line is 1-888-409-0141, and anyone can call or text 988.

Will my employer know?

No. Honest Club is independent and doesn't report to any hospital or health system. What you say stays between us, with the usual exception if someone's safety is at risk.

How long does it last?

As long as it's useful. There's no fixed package, and you can stop whenever you're ready. We talk through packages and pricing on the free call.
